# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owners

### Role Summary
Product Owners translate business priorities into clear delivery expectations and backlog decisions. They work with the Product Manager to ensure the team is building the right outcomes, and with the delivery team to clarify scope, sequencing, and acceptance.

### Responsibilities
- Define and prioritize backlog items with business context
- Clarify requirements, trade-offs, and acceptance criteria for the team
- Act as the primary decision-maker for product scope within agreed boundaries
- Align delivery decisions with customer value and business goals
- Coordinate with stakeholders to confirm changes in priority or direction

### Goals
- Keep the team focused on the highest-value outcomes
- Reduce ambiguity in scope and decision-making
- Improve delivery predictability and business alignment

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and story definition sessions
- Acceptance reviews and milestone alignment
- Product and stakeholder updates during planning and release readiness

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to align strategy, outcomes, and prioritization
- Collaborates with Project Managers on sequencing, dependencies, and reporting
- Coordinates with Developers and QA/Test Leads to clarify user value and acceptance criteria
- Provides Product Manager and stakeholders with clear decisions when trade-offs are needed

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts connect stakeholder needs, process understanding, and implementation details. They help reduce ambiguity by documenting requirements, validating assumptions, and tracing business value through delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements, workflows, and constraints
- Translate stakeholder needs into clear functional requirements and acceptance criteria
- Analyze gaps, process inefficiencies, and improvement opportunities
- Support data and reporting needs for decision-making
- Help maintain traceability from business goals to delivered outcomes

### Goals
- Improve requirement quality and business clarity
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or inconsistent expectations
- Strengthen communication between business and delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Functional specification reviews and acceptance walkthroughs
- Updates to process maps, business rules, and change requests

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers and Product Owners on opportunity framing, problem statements, and prioritization signals
- Works with Project Managers to identify cross-team impacts and dependencies
- Supplies requirements context to Developers, QA/Test Leads, and Security/Privacy stakeholders
- Helps confirm that delivered work meets intended business value and operational needs

---

## Technical Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects define the technical direction, design decisions, and system constraints that keep delivery safe and sustainable. They ensure that solutions align with quality, maintainability, and operational needs.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture, design patterns, and technical trade-offs
- Review technical dependencies, risks, and non-functional requirements
- Support implementation decisions and technical standards
- Help plan milestone readiness, integration points, and rollback considerations
- Coordinate with operations and security roles on compliance and platform readiness

### Goals
- Deliver architecture that supports long-term stability and scale
- Reduce technical debt and avoid late design surprises
- Ensure solutions can be supported reliably after launch

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical risk assessments and dependency planning
- Release and operational readiness checkpoints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Project Managers on schedule, risk management, and dependency tracking
- Works with Developers to shape implementation quality and maintainability standards
- Provides input to Product Owners and Business Analysts on feasibility, trade-offs, and constraints
- Coordinates with Security/Privacy and Operations/Service Owners on compliance, resilience, and supportability

---

## QA and Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA and Test Leads define the quality strategy, testing approach, and evidence needed to validate that solutions meet the intended outcomes and regulatory or operational requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, test plans, and quality gates
- Review requirements and acceptance criteria for testability
- Coordinate test execution, regression coverage, and defect triage
- Communicate release readiness, defects, and quality risks to stakeholders
- Partner with delivery teams to improve quality throughout the lifecycle

### Goals
- Reduce defects reaching production or end users
- Improve confidence in readiness for release and adoption
- Support continuous quality and learning across the project

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions and quality reviews
- Defect triage and release readiness meetings
- Quality metrics and product risk updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers and Product Owners to validate business value and acceptance criteria
- Partners with Business Analysts to confirm requirements are testable and complete
- Escalates release or risk concerns to Project Managers and Product Owners when quality gaps affect commitments
- Coordinates with Security/Privacy and Operations roles to confirm deployment and support readiness

---

## Change and Adoption Leads

### Role Summary
Change and Adoption Leads help ensure that new processes, tools, or capabilities are understood, accepted, and used effectively by stakeholders and end users.

### Responsibilities
- Develop change plans, communications, and training strategies
- Identify user impacts, adoption barriers, and readiness needs
- Coordinate stakeholder engagement and feedback loops during rollout
- Measure adoption progress and surface areas requiring support
- Support transition from delivery to steady-state operations

### Goals
- Reduce resistance to change and improve adoption outcomes
- Improve user confidence in new workflows and tools
- Support a smooth transition to ongoing operations

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and training sessions
- Readiness and adoption check-ins
- Feedback summaries and change impact updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers on communications and rollout sequencing
- Coordinates with Product Owners and Business Analysts to align messages with user needs and benefits
- Collaborates with Developers and Technical Leads on the operational impact of changes
- Shares readiness and support considerations with Operations/Service Owners

---

## Operations and Service Owners

### Role Summary
Operations and Service Owners focus on the ongoing support, monitoring, and service continuity required after delivery. They help ensure that solutions can be supported and maintained without causing operational disruption.

### Responsibilities
- Define support models, monitoring requirements, and incident ownership
- Review operational readiness before launch or transition
- Establish runbooks, service checks, and escalation paths
- Coordinate with teams on maintenance, support, and continuity planning
- Maintain service quality and stakeholder confidence after go-live

### Goals
- Ensure high service reliability and clear ownership after launch
- Improve operational readiness and reduced support friction
- Balance delivery speed with sustainable supportability

### Typical Communication
- Operational readiness reviews and handoff meetings
- Support escalations and service health updates
- Runbook and incident response coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Technical Leads on architecture, monitoring, supportability, and performance expectations
- Partners with Project Managers to align handoff schedules and operational dependencies
- Inputs into release and rollout planning with Product Owners and Change/Adoption Leads
- Uses QA/Test evidence and business requirements to confirm the service is ready for use

---

## Security and Privacy Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Stakeholders review risks, controls, and compliance obligations so that projects meet required protection standards without slowing down delivery unnecessarily.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Review architecture, workflows, and data handling for risks
- Support control design, testing, and evidence collection
- Escalate risks and recommend mitigations where needed
- Help confirm that the solution is ready for deployment and ongoing operations

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy risk throughout the lifecycle
- Preserve trust with stakeholders and users
- Support compliant and defensible project decisions

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and design assessments
- Privacy and compliance checkpoints
- Risk registers and mitigation updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Reviews technical designs with Technical Leads and Architects
- Helps Business Analysts and Product Owners understand compliance constraints and user impacts
- Provides quality and readiness input to QA/Test Leads and Project Managers
- Works with Operations/Service Owners to maintain secure, compliant support practices after launch

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Where team size is small, multiple responsibilities may be combined into a single person, but accountability should still be explicit.
- This structure is intended to clarify who is responsible for decision-making, delivery, quality, transition, and operational support across the project lifecycle.
